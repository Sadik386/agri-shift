- **Audio:** Clear microphone with noise suppression enabled.
- **Mouse Cursor:** Move deliberately and smoothly; avoid rapid erratic mouse shaking.

## 🎙️ Complete Voiceover & Screenplay

---

### Segment 1: The Hook & Core Vision (0:00 – 0:15)
> **Screen Action:** Start on the landing page [https://agri-shift.vercel.app/](https://agri-shift.vercel.app/). Smoothly scroll past the hero atmosphere banner.

**Voiceover:**  
> *"Welcome to AgriShift. In Bangladesh, climate disasters and unsustainable flood-irrigated Boro rice are depleting groundwater tables by up to 0.75 meters every year. AgriShift bridges NASA's Earth observation constellation directly into actionable, low-cost crop diversification for millions of farmers."*

---

### Segment 2: Climate Disaster & Cost Resilience Engine (0:15 – 0:32)
> **Screen Action:** Focus on the `Disaster Resilience & Low-Cost Crop Engine` card. Click the **"Drought"** tab, then switch to **"Flash Flood"**. Drag the land size slider from 5 acres to 10 acres to show live cost savings updating. Click the **Language Toggle** in the header to demonstrate instant bilingual translation.

**Voiceover:**  
> *"Our primary vision begins with climate disaster analysis. Instead of blind farming, our engine assesses localized risks—like drought or flash floods—and recommends resilient, low-input crops such as Sorghum and Mungbean. With dynamic cost modeling, farmers can see exact diesel, fertilizer, and irrigation savings per acre in real-time—fully accessible in both Bangla and English."*

---

### Segment 3: 3D Mission Control & Orbital Telemetry (0:32 – 0:48)
> **Screen Action:** Click **"LAUNCH MISSION CONTROL"** to go to `/dashboard` (or click `1. GLOBE VIEW` & `2. FIELD MAP`). Click and rotate the **3D Orbital Earth Globe**, showing SMAP, Landsat, and ECOSTRESS orbits. Then show the **3D Field Polygon Terrain** and toggle between **"Root moisture"** and **"Canopy height"**.

**Voiceover:**  
> *"Entering our 3D Mission Control dashboard, Module 1 fuses six satellite streams: SMAP soil moisture, Landsat 9 NDVI, and ECOSTRESS surface temperatures. In Module 2, high-resolution DEM elevation models extrude the parcel into 3D volumetric moisture columns, revealing dry zones before the farmer even steps into the field."*

---

### Segment 4: 3-Year Adaptive Rotation & 3D Root Simulation (0:48 – 1:05)
> **Screen Action:** Scroll to **Module 03 (3-Year Crop Rotation Planner)**. Click on **"Year 2"** (Mungbean). Click the **"PLAY CYCLE"** button on the 3D Soil Profile viewer to watch the root system grow dynamically through the stratigraphy layers. Point to the **"+38% WATER SAVED"** green badge.

**Voiceover:**  
> *"Module 3 solves long-term soil degradation. Our 3-Year Adaptive Rotation sequence replaces continuous rice with Maize, Sorghum, and nitrogen-fixing Mungbean. Farmers can interactively simulate seasonal root growth down to 100 centimeters inside the 3D subsoil stratigraphy, breaking nematode cycles and saving up to 7 billion litres of aquifer water regionally."*

---

### Segment 5: Financial Volatility & 3D Profit Surface (1:05 – 1:20)
> **Screen Action:** Click **`TELEMETRY ANALYTICS`** (`/analytics`). Click **"Expand 3D Surface"** to reveal the colorful 3D Profit Landscape. Drag to rotate the surface. Adjust the **"Fertilizer Global Price Index"** slider to demonstrate how the surface adapts to cost shocks.

**Voiceover:**  
> *"Agronomy must be profitable. Module 5 features an interactive 3D Monte Carlo profit surface, running 2,500 stochastic simulations against rainfall deficits and global fertilizer spikes. Farmers can calculate exact drought break-even points and secure positive returns even during severe climate shocks."*

---

### Segment 6: Offline Embedded NASA Datasets & 104-Hex Heatmap (1:20 – 1:35)
> **Screen Action:** Click **`NASA DATASETS`** (`/datasets`) to show the **"OFFLINE LOCAL REPOSITORY"** badge and export options. Then click **`HEATMAP`** (`/heatmap`) to reveal the 104-hexagon trend grid across Bangladesh.

**Voiceover:**  
> *"Crucially, AgriShift is 100% offline-ready. Seven NASA satellite mission datasets are cached in-browser for zero-internet field operations, while our 104-hex heatmap applies 40 years of Mann-Kendall climate trend tests across all 64 districts."*

---

### Segment 7: LangChain AI Agronomist & Mission Control Chatbot (1:35 – 1:54)
> **Screen Action:** Click the floating **"Ask AgriShift AI ◈"** badge (or top navigation **`7. ASK AI (LANGCHAIN)`** at `/chat`). Select **"Rajshahi"** in the district dropdown. Click the prompt chip: *"Drought crops for Rajshahi?"*. Show the streaming response citing NASA SMAP 0–5 cm moisture and recommending Sorghum with diesel savings. Open Settings to show Google Gemini 3.8 Flash and Offline Engine toggles.

**Voiceover:**  
> *"New in AgriShift is our LangChain-powered AI Agronomist. Powered by Gemini 3.8 Flash and strict RAG grounding, the assistant fuses real-time telemetry from all 7 NASA missions to provide instant, district-level farming advice. Whether online with high-speed LLMs or running fully offline in remote villages, farmers receive actionable agronomy guidance in seconds."*

---

### Segment 8: Outro & Call to Action (1:54 – 2:00)
> **Screen Action:** Smoothly glide back to the top bar with the **AgriShift** logo and live URL.

**Voiceover:**  
> *"AgriShift: Bringing NASA space telemetry and artificial intelligence to grassroots agriculture. Explore live at agri-shift.vercel.app. Thank you!"*
